Step 2 — Mount uploads in index.ts (safest place)

This avoids further edits to routes.ts and keeps uploads nicely isolated.

In server/index.ts:

Ensure static hosting (you said you added it, keep it):

// ...
app.use(
  "/uploads",
  express.static(path.join(process.cwd(), "public/uploads"), {
    immutable: true,
    maxAge: "365d",
  })
);


Mount the uploads router with rate limiting and optional auth:

import uploadsRouter from "./routes/uploads";
import { limitUploads } from "./middleware/rateLimitUploads";

// Mount before your general 404/error handlers:
