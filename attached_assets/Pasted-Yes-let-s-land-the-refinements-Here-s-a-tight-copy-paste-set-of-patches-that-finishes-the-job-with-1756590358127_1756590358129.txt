Yes—let’s land the refinements. Here’s a tight, copy-paste set of patches that finishes the job without touching your working layout.

1) Text token cleanup (light/dark correct contrast)
One-time find/replace (safe codemod)

Run these in your editor search (regex on):

Replace titles/headings

Find: className="([^"]*)text-white/90([^"]*)"

Replace: className="$1dark:text-white$2 text-foreground"

Replace general white text on light surfaces

Find: text-white(?:\/\d+)?

Replace (manual review where needed): text-foreground dark:text-white

Replace “muted” white text

Find: text-slate-200|text-zinc-200|text-white/60

Replace: text-muted-foreground dark:text-white/60

Concrete examples (drop into components)

Section header

- <h2 className="text-white/90 font-semibold">Recent Incidents</h2>
+ <h2 className="text-foreground dark:text-white font-semibold">Recent Incidents</h2>


Card title

- <h3 className="text-white/90 text-base">{incident.title}</h3>
+ <h3 className="text-foreground dark:text-white text-base">{incident.title}</h3>


Meta row (time, tags)

- <span className="text-white/60">{ago}</span>
+ <span className="text-muted-foreground dark:text-white/60">{ago}</span>


Buttons/links in sheet header

- <Button variant="ghost" className="text-white/80 hover:text-white">
+ <Button variant="ghost" className="text-foreground/90 hover:text-foreground dark:text-white/90 dark:hover:text-white">
