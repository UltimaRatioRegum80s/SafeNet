Great—those two are “utility” actions (Locate & Incidents). Best spot that avoids the Report FAB, top bar, and attribution:
✅ Recommendation: Bottom-center mini dock (horizontal)
Why: clear of the Report FAB (bottom-right), easy thumb reach, doesn’t fight the top menu, and stays above the attribution bar.


Placement: left:50% + translateX(-50%), bottom: env(safe-area-inset-bottom) + 72px (tweak 64–88px based on your attribution height).


Drop-in CSS/TSX
// FABStack.tsx (new mini dock for the two buttons)
<div
  className="
    pointer-events-auto
    absolute z-[54] left-1/2 -translate-x-1/2
    bottom-[calc(env(safe-area-inset-bottom)+72px)]
    flex items-center gap-3
  "
>
  <button
    className="h-12 w-12 rounded-full bg-foreground/80 text-white shadow-lg
               backdrop-blur-md dark:bg-foreground/70"
    aria-label="Locate me"
    onClick={onLocate}
  >
    <TargetIcon className="h-5 w-5 mx-auto" />
  </button>

  <button
    className="h-12 w-12 rounded-full bg-foreground/80 text-white shadow-lg
               backdrop-blur-md dark:bg-foreground/70"
    aria-label="Open incidents"
    onClick={onToggleList}
  >
    <ListIcon className="h-5 w-5 mx-auto" />
  </button>
</div>

  {/* buttons */}

Alternatives (if you prefer)
Right-center vertical (top:50%, right:16px) — super clean on tablets; slightly less thumb-friendly on tall phones.


Bottom-left (mirror of current) — safe from Report FAB, but watch the Leaflet attribution; add bottom: calc(env(safe-area-inset-bottom)+72px).


I’d implement the bottom-center dock first; it consistently reads well in your screenshots for iPhone/Pixel/Samsung sizes.
